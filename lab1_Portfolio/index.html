About Me

My introduction

I am a Computer Engineering student with a strong interest in understanding software systems end to end. Rather than limiting myself to a single area early on, I actively explore different parts of the software stack to build a solid foundation and a clear understanding of how modern applications are designed, built, and maintained.


My projects reflect hands-on learning across frontend and web fundamentals, including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, and Tailwind CSS. I enjoy building small but complete applications such as interactive web apps, games, and utilities, focusing on clean structure, usability, and practical problem solving. These projects help me strengthen my core concepts while gradually improving my development workflow.


Alongside web development, I work with Python, SQL, and Jupyter Notebooks, exploring numerical methods, data handling, and analytical thinking. This has helped me develop a problem-solving mindset and an appreciation for data-driven approaches, bridging the gap between theoretical concepts and real implementations.


My GitHub serves as a learning log — a place where I experiment, practice, and refine my skills through real code rather than tutorials alone. I value consistency, curiosity, and building things from scratch, even if they start small. Currently, I am focused on strengthening my full-stack fundamentals while gradually expanding into backend systems, databases, and data-oriented domains.


I am motivated by continuous improvement and long-term growth, with the goal of becoming a well-rounded software engineer who understands both the technical depth and the bigger picture of software development.
